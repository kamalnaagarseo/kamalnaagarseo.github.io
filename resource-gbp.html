GBP TASKS & QUALITY CHECKS

A Practical Google Business Profile Task Checklist

Practical reference notes for teams managing recurring SEO execution.

← All resources

Start with accurate business information

Review business name, address or service-area setup, phone number, category choices, and official site link. Keep each field aligned with the verified business details.

Organize changes before making them

Collect service changes, descriptions, photos, questions, and other edits in one review list. Confirm that supplied details are accurate and appropriate before updating a profile.

Use a repeatable review process

Record the requested change, the person responsible, and whether it has been reviewed. Keep client approvals and platform policy checks in the same workflow.

Report what was actually completed

Make a distinction between an update being submitted, accepted, visible, and any separately measured search performance. Avoid claiming ranking improvements without current evidence.
