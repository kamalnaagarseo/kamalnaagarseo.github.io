Local SEO checklist

A simple GBP support checklist for agencies.

Organize the important inputs and reviews before implementing Google Business Profile-related tasks.

Confirm the basics

Record the business’s verified name, address where applicable, phone number, website, and relevant operating details. Do not invent or alter business details to target extra locations.

Review profile content

  • Check primary and additional categories against actual services.
  • Review business description and service information for accuracy.
  • Confirm hours, special hours, and service areas with the business.
  • Check photos and other media for ownership and appropriateness.
  • Review relevant profile updates that the agency has approved.

Make the process reviewable

Capture proposed edits before implementation when approval is required. Record any changes made, questions needing business confirmation, and follow-up tasks.

Keep expectations realistic

A completed profile checklist is not a guarantee of ranking improvements. Local visibility depends on many factors, and any performance claim should be backed by actual measurement.
