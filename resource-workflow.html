Agency operations

How to hand off backend SEO tasks clearly.

A practical reference for agency owners and SEO managers who delegate implementation work.

Start with the purpose of the task

Write down the website or profile being worked on and the reason the task matters. A request such as “update these pages” is less useful than a request that identifies the pages, objective, and expected output.

Include the information needed to execute

  • Website or profile URL and the exact pages in scope.
  • The agency’s approved keywords, content brief, or reference material.
  • Required access and what should not be changed.
  • Priority order, due date, and expected deliverable format.
  • Review or approval steps before anything goes live.

Define completion before work starts

For example, an on-page SEO update might be complete once titles and headings are implemented, affected pages are checked on mobile, and a change log is delivered. Clear completion criteria reduce rework.

Keep an audit trail

A shared task tracker can record what changed, what is pending, and where the agency needs to make a decision. Avoid placing passwords in a task document. Use an approved secure access method instead.

Protect the client relationship

When work is white-label, the agency remains the main contact for the client. Confirm boundaries around direct communication, publication, and approval at the outset.
